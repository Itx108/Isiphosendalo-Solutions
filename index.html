About us

Technology built around real operational needs.

Isiphosendalo Solutions is a South African technology business focused on building useful, maintainable software for businesses and organisations.

We work beyond basic websites by developing systems that can include authentication, role-based access, databases, bookings, payments, dashboards, offline-ready experiences and other workflows needed to operate a real service.

Our approach

Understand the workflow, design the solution, build the system, deploy it, then improve it through real-world use and feedback.
